Keep the devlog usable at 40+ entries

Three ways to make /builds/:slug shorter and easier to scan. Each one uses vizBot's real 43 entries and photos, the section's current purple styles (the real buildlog.css is inlined), and keeps the page top exactly as it is today. Every comp works at phone width.

TodayvizBot's devlog renders all 43 entries in full (about 5,100 words). The devlog alone is about 21,000 px tall on a laptop and 28,000 px on a phone.
PhotosEach photo is its own 220 px figure in a 360 px-wide column, so an entry with 3 photos adds about 750 px of mostly empty width.
VideoEvery mp4 in the devlog autoplays inline (about 5 MB each). All three comps show the poster with a play button and play it in the lightbox.

recommended

A · Ledger

It does the most for length: the devlog goes from about 21,000 px to about 3,100 px (newest entry open), or 2,400 px with every entry closed. It keeps the spine, the dates and the tag chips, so it still looks like today's page. The same row also works for /builds/recently (139 entries), which has the same problem and is worse. A

Ledger

most compactalso /recently

Every entry is one row on the purple spine (date · title · tags · up to 3 small thumbs) and opens in place. Month markers on the spine show the counts. The newest entry starts open.

Density
About 46 px per closed row on a laptop and about 75 px on a phone. The devlog is about 3,100 px with the newest entry open (today it is about 21,000 px).
Roll-up
Each entry opens and closes on its own (a native <details>). Expand all / Collapse all. A #e-… link opens one entry directly.
Photos
A mosaic: 1 photo is wide, 2 sit side by side, 3 or more show one large photo and two small ones, with “+N more” on the last. The lightbox steps through all of them.
Tradeoff
Closed, you see no text at all, so you have to open a row to read it. Good for scanning, but you click more to read in order.

B

Chapters

browse by month

Entries are grouped by month. A closed month is one band showing its counts, its photos, the first few titles and its top tags. Open months show the entries as two columns of cards, with each card's text clamped. A sticky month bar jumps to any month.

Density
All 9 months closed come to about 1,700 px. By default (October open, 20 entries) it is about 5,400 px, and about 9,800 px with every month open, because two columns hold about twice as many entries per screen.
Roll-up
By month (the newest month starts open). Open all / Close all, and a Full text switch for the clamped cards.
Photos
One lead photo with a row of thumbs under it. Tap a thumb to swap the lead; tap the lead to open the lightbox.
Tradeoff
Drops the timeline spine, so the cards read in a zigzag. Months are uneven (October has 20 entries, April 1), so one open month can still be long.

C

Teaser timeline

smallest change

Today's timeline, kept as it is. Each entry shows only its first paragraph, with “Read the rest · N words”. Photos sit in one strip of short tiles. The list shows 10 entries at a time, using the same “Show more” button as WIP Pix.

Density
The first 10 entries take about 2,650 px. All 43 as teasers come to about 11,400 px, about half of today's height.
Roll-up
Each entry's text opens on its own, with Expand all / Collapse all text. Older entries load in pages of 10, or all at once with Show all.
Photos
One horizontal strip of 140 px tiles with one-line captions. It scrolls sideways (with snap) on a phone.
Tradeoff
The least work and the least change, but it still runs long once you page through, and older entries are not on the page until you load them.
