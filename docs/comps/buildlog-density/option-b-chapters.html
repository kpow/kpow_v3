What this option changes
  • Entries roll up by month. A closed month is one band: name, counts, a strip of its photos, and the first few titles.
  • The newest month starts open. Open months show entries as a two-column card grid (one column on a phone).
  • Each card clamps its text to about six lines with “Read more”. Toolbar: Open all months / Close all, and Full text on/off.
  • Photos: a lead image with a thumbnail row under it; tap a thumb to swap the lead, tap the lead for the lightbox.
  • A sticky month bar jumps straight to any month (and opens it).
