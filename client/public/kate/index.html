1

Update the brain

about 5 minMac · Terminal

The brain is the program on your Mac that listens and talks for vizBot. One command gets the new version.

You'll know it worked when

Terminal shows no message about a problem, and vizBot still answers when you tap his head and say hi.

If it doesn't

"Your install link isn't active any more"
Your link expired. Ask Kevin for a fresh one, then run vizbrain update again.
"Couldn't reach the install site"
Check the Mac's internet, wait a minute, run it again.

2

Update vizBot himself

about 5 minMac · web browser

New firmware lets him show album covers and listen for your answer after he asks a question.

You'll know it worked when

His web page, in the Device section, says Firmware v3.7.36, and his head moves when he wakes up.

If it doesn't

"Upload failed, check board type and file"
You picked another board's file or the -factory one. Download the Stackchan file again and retry.
His page won't open
Make sure the Mac is on the home Wi-Fi (not a guest network), and wait 30 seconds after he turns on.
His head doesn't move
Do the 6-second hold again, then turn him on.

3

Let your Mac talk to Spotify

about 10 minMac · Terminal + browser

This is the longest part. You install a small Spotify helper, then give it one approval so vizBot can edit playlists and like songs. It uses the same Spotify app your vizSpot already uses, so there's nothing new to create.

You'll know it worked when

The browser says "spotify-mcp is signed in. You can close this tab." and Terminal says "Signed in."

If it doesn't

command not found: uv or spotify-mcp
Close Terminal, open a new Terminal window, and run the same line again.
Spotify says "redirect_uri: Not matching configuration"
The address isn't saved on that app yet. Redo steps 3 to 5. Check you clicked Save, and that the address has no space or slash at the end. Then run spotify-mcp login again.
The login can't find your vizSpot
Check that vizspot.local opens. If not, power-cycle the vizSpot and try again.
Part 3Your Mac can play, queue, like and add to playlists. The hard part is behind you.

4

Plug Spotify into vizBot

about 3 minMac · Terminal

One command tells vizBot's brain to use the Spotify helper. The second checks it.

You'll know it worked when

The first line starts spotify: ready and says 10 tools, followed by a list of them.

If it doesn't

It says anything other than ready
Run spotify-mcp status. It says where the Spotify sign-in comes from. If it mentions logging in, redo the last step of Part 3.
Part 4vizBot has Spotify. Time to play.
5

Try it out

about 5 minvizBot + his web page

The fun part. Open the Spotify app on your Mac first, so he has somewhere to play.

You'll know it worked when

Music plays, covers flash, and he hears your answer without a tap.

If it doesn't

He says nothing is playing, or can't find a device
Open the Spotify app on the Mac and press play on anything once, then ask again.
He says Spotify needs Premium
Playing and skipping need Spotify Premium on your account.
No cover appears
Check Part 2's firmware number says v3.7.36.
