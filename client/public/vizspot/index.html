Before you start

You'll need Spotify Premium and about 5 minutes, all on this phone.

Your board talks to Spotify through a small personal app that you create. Spotify only lets Premium accounts create one.

Create your Spotify app

  1. Open Spotify for Developers and sign in with the Spotify account you listen on. Accept the terms, and confirm your email if it asks.
  2. Tap Create app.
  3. App name: vizSpot. App description: My vizSpot board.
  4. Redirect URI: copy this exactly, paste it in, then tap Add.
  5. Tick Web API, tick the terms box, and tap Save.

Copy your Client ID

In your new app, open Settings and copy the Client ID: 32 letters and numbers.

Not the Client secret. You'll never need that.

Sign in to Spotify

Tap Connect, then Agree on Spotify's page. You'll come straight back here.

If Spotify shows Invalid redirect URI, recheck step 4 of the setup. If it says Invalid client, recheck the Client ID.
