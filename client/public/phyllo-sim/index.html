What you can use

Your code runs once per LED, every frame. Return [r, g, b] (0–255).

  • led.i spiral index (0 = center)
  • led.chain position on the data line
  • led.r 0–1 center to edge
  • led.theta radians, led.angle degrees
  • led.nx, led.ny −1…1, led.x, led.y mm
  • led.arm 0…F−1, led.arm2 0…G−1 (the two Fibonacci arm families)
  • led.armRank arms in clockwise order around the board
  • led.n, led.F, led.G counts
  • t seconds, s state that persists (s.dt, s.frame)
  • hsv(h, s, v) h in turns (0–1 wraps), s and v 0–1
  • clamp(x, a, b), mix(a, b, k), fract(x), noise(x, y, z) 0–1
